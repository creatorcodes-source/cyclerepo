Built for Our Roads, Not European Runways

Imported bikes often fail on Sri Lankan potholes, monsoons, and spare-part shortages. Veloce is purpose-built to solve these realities.

Tropical Monsoon Sealing

Corrosion-proof stainless hardware and fully enclosed internal routing keep your bottom bracket and headset silky smooth through wet monsoons.

Pothole-Tested Wheelsets

Reinforced double-wall alloy and carbon rims with 32c-45c tubeless tire clearance absorb asphalt craters and rough gravel without buckled wheels.

Local Parts Guarantee

Never wait 3 months for an imported derailleur hanger or proprietary headset. 100% of wear-and-tear spares are stocked in Colombo with next-day islandwide delivery.

Fair Direct Pricing

By cutting out unnecessary middle-tier importers, we deliver high-spec Shimano and disc brakes at honest, transparent prices in Sri Lankan Rupees.
